Eufy - Camera

One Lovelace card for a pan-tilt eufy camera: the live picture, a d-pad that really moves it, and your saved positions a click away.

Install GitHub Releases

What you get

🕹️ A d-pad that moves the camera

Drag the joystick, or hold a direction and it keeps stepping. Three shapes: joystick, flat ring, or four keys in a cross.

📍 Presets as buttons

One chip per position the camera has actually stored, read from the camera itself. Click, and it goes there.

🔋 The live view, on your terms

It shows the last event's picture and opens the stream only when you press play — then closes itself. On a battery camera that is the difference between days and hours.

🔎 Nothing to look up

Pick the camera from a list. The controls behind it are found for you, and survive a rename or a change of language.

Install

  1. Add the repository to HACS — one click:
    or by hand: HACS → ⋮ → Custom repositories, add https://github.com/Filpin011/eufy-camera-card with type Dashboard
  2. Search HACS for Eufy - Camera and download it
  3. Reload the browser with Ctrl+Shift+R

Releases are currently marked pre-release. If HACS shows nothing to download, open the card in HACS and turn on Show beta versions in its ⋮ menu.

Use it

Add the card, pick your camera, done — the visual editor covers everything: 

type: custom:eufy-camera-card
device: Front door camera
camera: auto

You will also need

Project What for
ha-eufy-sdk the integration this card drives, plus a pan-tilt camera
webrtc-camera the live view — without it the card shows the still picture and skips the player
